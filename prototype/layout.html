Camera aggregation rules

Sections link to any number of configured camera-zone pairs. Their live person count uses the maximum fresh camera-zone count, never an unverified sum. Partial dwell and observed-track counts come from one supporting camera. Across camera feeds, duplicate shoppers cannot be identified without validated calibration and synchronization.

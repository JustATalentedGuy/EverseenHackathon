Where did people spend observed time?

Each square is 1/16 of camera width × 1/9 of camera height. Stronger colors mean more continuously observed tracked presence, not necessarily more unique shoppers.

Blue: 0–0.25Green: 0.25–0.5Amber: 0.5–1Red: 1+

Color scale: average tracked people per image square during the selected period. An uncolored square means no *verified continuous* presence, not guaranteed absence. This fixed scale does not change between camera frames.

Start monitoring to calculate observed person-time.

Example: 1 person continuously visible in the same square for 10 of the observed 20 seconds = 10 person-seconds, or 0.5 average people in that square. Tracking gaps are excluded; no physical floor area, sales or unique visitors are inferred.

Measurement integrity
Tracks are camera-local, and “track entries” can repeat when a tracker loses somebody. Dwell is accrued only between reliable successive sightings. No full-store unique visitors, completed checkouts or cross-camera identity matching are claimed without appropriate verification.
